# Online Retail Analysis

---

## Executive Summary
A UK online giftware retailer wants to know how the business has performed over the past two years and where it should be focusing next. To answer this, I used SQL in jupyterlab notebook through DuckDB to clean and analyse the dataset and Power BI and Claude AI to create visualisations. After identifying that revenue growth came from larger orders rather than more of them, I looked at where that revenue sits. A small group of customers generates most of it and those with smaller first orders are the least likely to buy again. I recommend that the sales and marketing teams focus on:

1. Give the largest accounts a named contact in the sales teams who check in with them regularly so probem surface early rather than at the point of losing them
2. Follow up email within 2 weeks of first purchase for those with small first order purchase (Up to £167)
